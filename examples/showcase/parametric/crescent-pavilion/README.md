# Crescent pavilion

Curved oak ribs, bronze longitudinal ties, and a continuous stone walk are
separate stages in [design.json](design.json). The retained controls use a
6.2 m span, 4.1 m crown height, 11.5 m walk length, and 1.1 rad plan curvature.
[base.scene.json](base.scene.json) supplies the staging, lights, and cameras.

Follow the [shared prerequisites and commands](../README.md#reproduce-a-retained-study)
with `study=crescent-pavilion`. Inspect the silhouette, daylight between ribs,
and open inner edge. Compare the [wider revision](../crescent-pavilion-wide/README.md)
through the same camera.

Both files match the original MIT-licensed Slopcamera study at
[`63a0e3eed460fa80f1ae76983e9152c75a124392`](https://github.com/hraness/slopcamera/tree/63a0e3eed460fa80f1ae76983e9152c75a124392/examples/design).
They are a visual design study, not a certified structural design.
