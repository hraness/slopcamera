It includes cinematic, performance, effects, scene-authoring, parametric-design and image-gallery APIs, six portable operation codes, 17 MCP tools and eight built-in workflows. Each witness retains its actual execution revision, starting at `ba231ba8caa15d0ced48be1ec4d60a4124d9c24f`. Later static-overlay, RGB-blend, saved-splat color and cinema-renderer corrections require the identified source revision until included in a verified release. Shipped APIs do not establish the visual witnesses below; “Source only” describes missing evidence, not an unreleased API.

**Demonstrated** means the stated witness has reviewed evidence. **Partial** means real work exists but a specific observation remains open. **Planned** means no completed canonical witness; “source checked” still does not mean rendered. **Input needed** requires genuine authorized recording/audio. **Budget needed** requires a bounded live-model spend/provider choice and actual output qualification. No new-recording, measured narration, paid generation, or hosted-credit completion is implied.
