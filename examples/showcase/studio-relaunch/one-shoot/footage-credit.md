## Usage basis

[NASA's Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)
state that NASA content is generally not subject to copyright in the United
States and permit factual use that acknowledges NASA without implying its
endorsement. The commercial-use section also prohibits conveying NASA's
endorsement of goods or services. The reviewed catalog record lists no
third-party copyright credit.

These examples demonstrate independent editing of NASA footage. Their on-screen
credit reads “NASA footage · independent edit”; keep the source credit and this
usage record with derivatives. No NASA approval or endorsement is claimed.
NASA's footage is separate from the repository's MIT-licensed recipe and
original music; the repository license does not relicense that footage.
